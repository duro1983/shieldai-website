Human in the loop

Built to support staff, not replace them

The assistant absorbs repetitive enquiries so wait times and backlogs fall, particularly at peak. Staff stay responsible for complex, sensitive and escalated cases.

The assistant handles

Programme information and common questions, first-pass eligibility screening, after-hours enquiries and multilingual support.

Staff focus on

Complex eligibility cases, sensitive situations, escalated complaints and the policy decisions behind them.

The result

Shorter waits for citizens, less burnout on the front line, and more staff time on work that needs judgement.

How it works

From question to submitted application

Ask questions

Citizens ask about energy programmes in plain language. The assistant holds context across the conversation.

Check eligibility

Screening against home type, tenure, income and location, so nobody has to guess whether they qualify.

Apply

Guided application with form pre-filling, document upload assistance and submission tracking.

Track and analyse

A dashboard showing citizen demand, programme performance and where policy is creating friction.
