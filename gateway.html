Why Gateway

A compliance layer, not another model

Gateway sits between your applications and the AI providers you already want to use. The provider changes nothing about how it works — what changes is what reaches it, and what you can prove afterwards.

Privacy first

Automatic PII detection and masking. Sensitive data never reaches the AI provider, which is what makes PIPEDA compliance workable rather than aspirational.

Canadian data residency

All data processed and stored in Canada, so data-sovereignty requirements are met by deployment rather than by clause. Suited to government and healthcare.

Complete audit trail

Compliance reporting a regulator can read. Every request, every PII detection and every action is tracked, so you stay audit-ready between audits.

Enterprise scale

Handles millions of requests per day at low latency. Built for production workloads, not for a pilot that quietly stays a pilot.

Easy integration

A drop-in replacement for the OpenAI API. No code changes needed — you start in minutes rather than months.

Transparent pricing

Published tiers, published overage rates. No hidden fees and no surprises — you pay for what you use.

How it works

Four steps, and only one of them is yours

You change the endpoint. Gateway handles the compliance work on the way out and on the way back.

1

Your application

Send AI requests to ShieldAI instead of directly to the provider.

2

ShieldAI Gateway

We detect and mask personal information, then log the request for compliance.

3

AI provider

A clean, compliant request is processed by the model you chose.

4

Response

You get the result. We keep the record that shows how it was handled.
