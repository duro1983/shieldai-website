Ontario Energy Assistant

Citizen services

Helps Ontario residents discover, qualify for and apply to energy rebate programmes. It handles the repetitive share of enquiries — eligibility, application status, documentation — to reduce wait times and service backlogs. Human staff remain essential for complex, sensitive and escalated cases.

24/7 assistant covering 100+ languages

Real-time eligibility screening against programme rules

Guided application assistance, with escalation to staff

Analytics dashboard for programme and policy insight

Built against AODA and FIPPA

Who it is for
Government programme teams and energy administrators fielding high enquiry volume against a fixed headcount.
